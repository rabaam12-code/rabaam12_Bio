Hey, I'm Rabaam

Rabaam is my OC from Roblox — my virtual character, my digital self. I'm that person who's always down for a good time in the game, whether we're grinding for wins or just messing around.

When I'm not in Roblox, you'll find me hanging out in Discord, listening to music while talking random stuff with my friends. Late night calls, random laughs, sharing playlists — that's my vibe.
